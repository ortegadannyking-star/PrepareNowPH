ABOUT US

Building a Culture of Preparedness

PrepareNow PH is a student-led campaign that encourages people to understand disaster risks and prepare before emergencies happen.

Our Mission

To promote disaster preparedness by providing simple, useful, and accessible information that people can use before, during, and after disasters.

Our Vision

A community where people are informed, prepared, responsible, and ready to help one another during emergencies.

Our Objectives

Increase disaster awareness, encourage emergency planning, promote preparedness activities, and help communities become more resilient.

OUR GROUP

Students Working for a Safer Community

PrepareNow PH was created by students who want to promote disaster awareness and preparedness in their community.

Through this campaign, we share educational information, preparedness tips, activities, and helpful resources that can encourage people to take action before disasters happen.

COMMUNITY ISSUE

Why Disaster Preparedness Matters

Disasters can affect homes, schools, businesses, communities, and people's daily lives.

01

The Problem

Some people may not know what to do before, during, or after a disaster. Lack of preparation can make emergencies more difficult to handle.

02

Common Causes

Lack of awareness, limited emergency planning, misinformation, and failure to prepare supplies can increase community risks.

03

Possible Effects

Disasters can cause injuries, property damage, interruptions to education and work, displacement, and disruption of basic services.

04

Possible Solutions

Communities can improve preparedness through emergency plans, drills, information campaigns, go-bags, evacuation plans, and reliable information.

Get Information From Reliable Sources

Check official government agencies for weather, earthquake, volcanic, and disaster-related information.
