Emergency Guide

Before

  • ✔ Prepare a Go Bag
  • ✔ Secure heavy furniture
  • ✔ Know evacuation routes
  • ✔ Charge phones & power banks
  • ✔ Save emergency numbers

During

  • ✔ Stay calm
  • ✔ Drop, Cover and Hold
  • ✔ Stay away from windows
  • ✔ Follow authorities
  • ✔ Do not panic

After

  • ✔ Check for injuries
  • ✔ Expect aftershocks
  • ✔ Listen to official updates
  • ✔ Avoid damaged buildings
  • ✔ Help others safely
